# Crypto annual beta comparison

Same frozen stocks; cached monthly-updated weekly beta versus legacy daily beta.
SPY gross is prior-decision hedge weight times adjusted daily log return.
Costs inherit the input audit (25bps); turnover assumes this default.
Totals are additive, not compounded; final year is partial.
Regression beta/correlation are retrospective diagnostics, not estimators for trading.
No historical child caps/buffers, borrow, dividends or actual IB fills modeled.
Years and methods have been examined retrospectively; results are not untouched OOS.
Do not select beta solely to maximize SPY hedge PnL: hedging aims to manage exposure.
